About

Building with intention & craft

With 15 years of experience, I deliver enterprise applications across modern web technologies and the native Apple ecosystem, including iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, and visionOS, as well as hybrid mobile apps for iOS and Android.

I combine technical depth with strong UX instincts to build scalable, high-performance solutions. From front-end architecture and performance optimization to cross-platform development, I bring a holistic perspective to every project.

Currently leading a cross-functional team of engineers at Orthogonal, setting technical direction for Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) from architecture through delivery.

Today much of that work runs through AI: agentic development with AI coding agents, LLM-powered tooling and ML, and MCP servers that connect agents to the systems a team actually uses.
